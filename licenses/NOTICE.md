## Overture Maps places (includes Foursquare data)

- Overture Maps Foundation places, CDLA-Permissive-2.0 (LICENSE-CDLA-Permissive-2.0.txt) and
  Apache-2.0 (LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt). Foursquare notice: NOTICE-Foursquare.txt (full, unmodified).
- Credit lines as Overture asks (https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/): "Data from Overture Maps
  Foundation (Meta, Microsoft and others). Available under CDLA Permissive 2.0." and "Data from Foursquare. Copyright 2024 Foursquare Labs, Inc. All rights reserved. Available under Apache 2.0."
- Modification notice: Foursquare data was transformed to the Overture schema; modified by
  DriveMate: merged, de-duplicated, reduced to five fields.
- Places squares also contain OpenStreetMap points (ODbL) and OS Open Names (OGL), so they are
  labelled "ODbL, includes CDLA/Apache/CC0 data" (interpretation not verified by a lawyer).

## Mapillary

No Mapillary-derived value is published (owner, 18 Oct 2026: no licence grant has been established).
Detections may only be used inside DriveMate's own checks, to point out places for the owner to drive
and confirm; what the owner confirms is DriveMate's own survey.
