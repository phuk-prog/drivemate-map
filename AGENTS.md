# DriveMate map publisher instructions

Read [the DriveMate register](https://github.com/phuk-prog/drivemate-app/blob/main/REGISTER.md) first. Follow **1–24** and **1.1/1.2/2.1** numbering exclusively, with statuses Pending, In progress, Part done or Complete. Do not use a second order. Preserve map provenance, immutable releases, fail-closed publishing, tested rollback, licensing notices and official road-truth caution. Old workflow/test filenames are compatibility identifiers, not category numbers.
